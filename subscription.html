Hoekom 'n subskripsie? Why a subscription?

Blokraai Woordeboek is 'n volledig offline app met meer as 580 000 unieke leidraad–antwoord-kombinasies. Die databasis groei gereeld — nuwe kombinasies word ten minste een keer per maand bygevoeg. Jou subskripsie ondersteun die voortdurende ontwikkeling en onderhoud van hierdie databasis.

Blokraai Woordeboek is a fully offline app with over 580,000 unique clue–answer combinations. The database grows regularly — new combinations are added at least once a month. Your subscription supports the ongoing development and maintenance of this database.

Hoe dit werk

How it works

  1. Laai die app af vanaf Google Play en begin jou 7-dae gratis proefperiode outomaties.
  2. Download the app from Google Play and your 7-day free trial starts automatically.
  3. Gebruik al die funksies gedurende die proefperiode — die app werk volledig offline.
  4. Use all features during the trial — the app works fully offline.
  5. Kies 'n plan wanneer die proefperiode verstryk, direk in die app via Google Play.
  6. Choose a plan when the trial expires, directly in the app via Google Play.
  7. Bestuur of kanselleer jou intekening enige tyd in Google Play → Subskripsies.
  8. Manage or cancel your subscription anytime in Google Play → Subscriptions.

Herstel van aankope

Restoring purchases

As jy reeds 'n lewenslank-aankoop, basiese weergawe, bondel of aktiewe intekening het, herstel die app jou toegang outomaties wanneer jy dit oopmaak (via jou Google Play-rekening). Installeer die app op 'n nuwe toestel met dieselfde Google-rekening om voort te gaan.

If you already have a lifetime purchase, basic version, pack or active subscription, the app restores your access automatically when you open it (via your Google Play account). Install the app on a new device with the same Google account to continue.

Algemene vrae

Frequently asked questions

Het ek internet nodig?

Do I need internet?

Nee — soek en gebruik werk volledig offline. Internet word slegs benodig vir die eerste aflaai, app-opdaterings en aankope via Google Play.

No — searching and usage work fully offline. Internet is only needed for the initial download, app updates, and purchases via Google Play.

Wat gebeur as ek kanselleer?

What happens if I cancel?

By 'n maandelikse of jaarlikse intekening behou jy toegang tot die einde van die huidige faktuurperiode. Daarna sal jy 'n plan moet kies om die soekfunksies weer te gebruik.

With a monthly or yearly subscription you keep access until the end of the current billing period. After that you will need to choose a plan to use the search features again.

Is pryse op Google Play dieselfde?

Are prices on Google Play the same?

Pryse in die app word deur Google Play getoon en kan VAT insluit afhangend van jou rekening. Die bedrae hierbo is die basispryse (VAT uitgesluit) soos in die app vertoon word.

Prices in the app are shown by Google Play and may include VAT depending on your account. The amounts above are the base prices (excluding VAT) as displayed in the app.
